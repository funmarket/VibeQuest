## Approved open-first and current Vibe experience (2026-10-10)

Opening VibeQuest in a normal browser does not require login: visitors can navigate all five destinations and public pages, open permissible nested modals/cards and inspect public history, but not access private records. Mutating actions (including posts, comments, place validation, saving, follows, joining and financial requests) trigger **one contextual and dismissible sign-in/create-account flow** only when attempted; no fake guest balance or silent write replay. Genuine server-verified Telegram launch remains automatic canonical account resolution/provisioning under R38. See the [approved addendum](../product/OPEN_FIRST_LIVE_PLACES_FOUNDATION_DECISIONS_2026-10-10.md).

Place atmosphere uses **HighVibes** (energetic), **Vibing** (comfortable/relaxed), **LowVibes** (quiet/peaceful): all are valid, nonnegative ambience descriptions, not star ratings or permanent rankings. Authenticated users can report without compulsory GPS; adequately verified recent proximity may increase confidence, with privacy and abuse safeguards. A few-hour freshness window affects the current Vibe Meter; older moderated reports remain visible in history. Specific numerical window and formula are not yet approved. Keep factual Place validation separate from subjective atmosphere; Explore Vicinity and City Radar show actual evidence or honest low-data states. Vibe Trails are ordered, linked, town-scale day itineraries referencing canonical Places, not loose bookmarks. Preserve all nested modals and complete action states. None of this cancels Activities or official Events review mechanics.
